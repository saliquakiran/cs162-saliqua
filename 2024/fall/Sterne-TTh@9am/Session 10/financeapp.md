### Personal Finance Management App

## Application Description:

The "Personal Finance Management App" is designed to help users track their finances by managing multiple accounts, categorizing transactions, and monitoring their financial health. The app allows users to log income and expenses, generate reports on monthly spending, and monitor balances across different accounts.

## Key Features and Components:

1. User Profiles: Each user has an account to manage their finances.
2. Financial Accounts: Users can create and manage various accounts (e.g., savings, checking, credit cards) and track their balances.
3. Categories: Transactions are organized into categories (e.g., groceries, rent, income) to better understand spending patterns.
4. Transactions: Users log transactions (income or expense) tied to specific accounts and categories.
5. Reporting: Users can generate summaries of their spending, income, and remaining balances.

### Recipe Recommendation and Meal Planning App

## Application Description:

The "Recipe Recommendation and Meal Planning App" is designed to assist users with meal planning based on their dietary preferences and available ingredients. The app provides recipe recommendations, helps organize meal plans for specific days, and keeps track of ingredients and recipes. It supports users by generating grocery lists and allowing them to easily plan meals for the week.

## Key Features and Components:
1. Users: Manage personal profiles with login credentials.
Recipes: Store and manage a collection of recipes, including instructions and cook times.
2. Ingredients: Keep track of the ingredients needed for each recipe.
3. Meal Plans: Allow users to create meal plans for a specific date range, organizing recipes into daily meal schedules.
4. Recipe-Ingredient Mapping: Associates specific ingredients with the recipes they belong to, forming a many-to-many relationship between recipes and ingredients.
